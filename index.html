Property knowledge graph

Every document, owner, survey number, area and rule becomes a linked fact. A red link marks two facts that disagree. That is how a mismatch is caught in minutes, not days.

The valuer sees the exceptions first and signs once

The report is pre-filled. The valuer reviews only what the system flagged, then e-signs. The valuer stays the accountable signatory.

Business case: move the sliders and the numbers update

Today's cost includes the agency fee, re-visits, internal staff time and the cost of delay: interest income the bank does not earn while a sanctioned loan waits on its valuation.

Assumptions

Fixed: build cost ₹4.0 Cr, running cost ₹1.2 Cr a year, discount rate 12%, net interest margin 4%. Red cases make up the remainder.
